## Category-wide defects

- All 14 DNA blocks were the router template, each field ending in the same shared sentence ("render metal, glass, fabric, food surface, condensation, cosmetic gel, architecture finish …"), so a packshot and a real-estate photo carried identical instructions.
- **The same headphones appear on three presets** (SP01-050, SP01-082, SP01-087); packshot, product and e-commerce could not be told apart.
- SP01-056 "Architecture (Modern)" shows a timber cabin in a forest — not modern architecture.
- SP01-062 shows the inherited stock-office trope with generic smiling models.
- The category mixes profiles (flat lay, packshot, e-commerce) with lighting treatments and domain looks; the old DNA did not say which ones own the camera or backdrop.

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 14 (version 2). Each keeps the supplied object exactly (shape, proportions, color, supplied design) and states its own mechanism: softbox plus strip-light edges (product), infinity cove and shadowless sweep (packshot), razor strip lights on black (luxury macro), backlit frost (condensation), shift-lens verticals and hard sun on concrete (modern architecture), bracketed HDR from the corner (real estate). Flat lay, packshot and e-commerce say explicitly that they own the camera or backdrop, matching the review rule that flat lay changes the camera only when selected.
- Food Photography and Dark Chiaroscuro Food do not turn non-food subjects into food.
- Corporate Stock keeps its intentionally generic look; its briefs use humor (a wizard guild high-fiving over a crystal ball) so the card shows the look without real-looking stock models.
- 3 new briefs per preset, every hero object different (gauntlet, hourglass, potion bottle, great helm, ruby ring, frosted tankard …); no headphones, laptops or cars on wet streets.
- New presets (pending cards): Dark Chiaroscuro Food, Architectural Twilight Exterior, Hard-Light Glass Still Life, Lifestyle In-Hand Product, Color-Block Set Still Life, Ghost Mannequin Apparel. Category now 20.
- Overlap checked: Exploded View, Knolling and Caustics exist as CGI presets in pack_03; Water Splash exists as an FX in pack_09. The new photographic presets avoid those mechanisms or state the photographic difference (Hard-Light Glass Still Life vs CGI Caustics).

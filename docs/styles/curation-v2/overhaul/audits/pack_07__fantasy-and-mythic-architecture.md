Audited 2026-09-25 from the contact sheet (13 primaries, 1 variant) and full manifests. Review: kind `theme`; validation "a neutral structure keeps its function without receiving a fixed fantasy landmark".

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 13 (all eight fields + key features) with concrete construction, material and light mechanisms. Shared `subject_treatment` states the theme contract: keep the requested structure, its function, footprint and camera; never swap it for a castle, temple or landmark.
- Every preset has one brief that applies the theme to an ordinary modern building (library, turbine hall, petrol station, hospital, fire station, cinema lobby, laundromat, water tower, post office, dentist's clinic, football stadium, bakery, telephone exchange, hotel, ski lift station, apartment tower, farm shop, concert hall, airport hall, car park), proving the validation rule.
- Distinctions written into DNA: 054 is accreted megablock vs. SP07-006 Brutalist Architecture; 055 is building-scale candy vs. SP07-070 tabletop gingerbread; 058 hexagonal jewel crystal vs. 061 cyan bubbled ice; 057 riveted boiler-plate facades vs. SP07-050 glasshouse; 062 rope-lashed platforms vs. SP07-040 Arboreal Craft Shelter.
- Common avoids added: licensed fantasy locations, readable runes, mirror floors and waterfalls by default, swapping the structure for a castle or temple.
- No renames (no person, franchise or duplicate names).
- 7 new presets: **Rock-Hewn Colossus Facade**, **Tarred Stave-Hall Interlace**, **Cloud-Tier Lacquer Pagoda**, **Woven Willow Roundhouse**, **Chitin Carapace Architecture**, **Star-Lattice Desert Palace**, **Nautilus Chamber Architecture**. Overlaps checked: bone architecture (SP07-038 Ossuary Subterranean) and mudbrick (SP07-029 Adobe/Pueblo) exist, so they were not added; no stave, pagoda, wattle, chitin, lattice-screen or spiral-shell architecture preset exists.
- 60 briefs, no repeated subject; no people in any brief (inherited `person` negatives kept).

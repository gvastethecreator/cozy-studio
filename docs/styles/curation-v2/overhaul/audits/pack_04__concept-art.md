# pack_04 :: 4. Concept Art — audit

Audited 2026-09-25 from the contact sheet (29 primaries + 36 variants) and full manifests. The category already has 29 presets, so none were added.

## Findings

- All 29 DNA blocks were the router template ("acts as a transferable illustration router", "Transform any prompt subject through …"), and they listed tags instead of mechanisms. Every preset carried the same shared filler about "iteration rows, paintover marks, faction accents".
- The review rule ("the selected deliverable controls layout; a paint method alone does not") was not stated. Paint methods and deliverables (sheets, boards, strips, grids, maps) were written the same way.
- Cards: the same lone cloaked figure on a cliff appears in about 12 tiles (046-01, 047-01, 049-01, 055-01, 056-01, 058-01, 081-01, 082-01, 083-01, 085-01, 088, 093-01). A snowy cliffside village repeats in 046, 056, 058 and 082.
- Deliverables often render as single paintings: 085 color script, 093 lighting pass and 088 rough pass are one finished image each. 091 massing model is a finished dollhouse cutaway.
- Likeness risk: SP04-050-01 is a black armored car that reads as a known film vehicle, and the SP04-084-01 round droid reads as a known film robot. SP04-052 has a lighthouse (trope).
- Overlapping neighbours: 081, 087 and 097 were all "silhouette/thumbnail grids". 085 and 093 were both "color-script" passes. 051 and 089, 054 and 090, and 060 and 096 were not separated. 058 Low Poly Concept overlapped SP03-021 Low Poly.
- Name clash: SP04-046 "Speedpaint" duplicates SP06-047 in pack_06 Digital Art.
- Inherited negatives fought several techniques: `human` on creature design (needs a scale silhouette), `color` on storyboard (red arrows), `painted` on photobash, `photo`/`realistic` on the massing model (it is a photographed physical model), `finished`/`final` on the paintover demo, and `dead`/`desert`/`bare` on the foliage kit.

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 29 (8 fields + key features). There are two contracts. **Methods** (Value-Block Speedpaint, Matte Painting, Photobash, Low Poly Concept, Loose Gesture Energy Sketch, Rough Environment Pass, Architecture Massing Model) keep the requested subject, setting and camera and add no layout. **Deliverables** declare the layout they own.
- Separated neighbours:
  - 081 is a loose scatter of pose silhouettes (brush pen on toned paper). 087 is a rigid grid of outline variants in black on white. 097 shows one scene in twelve framings.
  - 085 is a strip of 8–12 frames following a story. 093 is a 2×2 grid of one fixed view under four lights.
  - 051 is one painted hero pose with anatomy insets. 089 is a graphite page of 6–9 variants.
  - 054 is one prop with hero, orthographic and zoom views. 090 is a lineup of 5–7 variants.
  - 060 is a strict side elevation with section slices. 096 is a row of 4–5 upgrade tiers.
  - 046 is a brush-led speedpaint. 088 is built from flat lasso-cut value planes. 049 is a navigable establishing view with a scale figure.
  - 056 is a finished photobash. 082 is a before/after paintover split.
  - 058 is a painted-over grey-box blockout, not the clean low-poly render in SP03-021.
  - 050 is a marker-on-grey-paper industrial sketch, and 092 a watercolor costume plate with swatches, distinct from the 048 orthographic turnaround.
- Renamed SP04-046 **Speedpaint → Value-Block Speedpaint** (duplicate name; old name kept as alias).
- `dropAvoid` removes the contradicting negatives listed above. New preset-specific negatives include known film vehicles or robots, a lone cloaked figure on a cliff edge, readable annotations and place names.
- 87 new briefs, no subject repeated, mostly dark medieval fantasy (plague city, reliquaries, necropolis, siege engines). Every brief names the deliverable layout or method marks. No cliffs with lone wanderers, lighthouses or franchise vehicles.

# pack_01 :: 4. Documentary And Street — audit

Audited 2026-09-25 from the contact sheet (9 primaries + 2 variants) and full manifests.

## Category-wide defects

- All 9 DNA blocks were the "transferable photographic style router" template, with one shared filler sentence per field ("grade as street, travel, concert, war, sport, paparazzi, or field documentary capture …") that listed every genre at once, so no preset had its own mechanism.
- The category mixes viewpoint profiles (GoPro, drone) with documentary treatments; the old DNA treated both the same.
- Cards repeat inherited tropes: a woman walking a bicycle on a wet street (SP01-046), a generic backpacker (SP01-070), generic stadium athletes (SP01-052).
- SP01-061's card shows a glamorous woman who reads as a real celebrity — a likeness risk for a paparazzi preset.

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 9 (version 2). GoPro Wide and Drone Aerial are profiles: they own the camera (150-degree fisheye with gear in frame; straight-down nadir) and keep the prompt's subject and setting. The other seven are treatments that witness the prompt without adding events, crowds or places; War forbids unrequested explosions and gore; Paparazzi requires fictional subjects and adds `real person likeness` to its negatives.
- 3 new briefs per preset (60 total), all fictional adults, no repeated subject in the category, no bicycles, trams, buses or wet markets.
- New presets (pending cards): Layered Color Street, Telephoto Compression Street, Hard-Shadow Street Geometry, Reflection-Layered Street, Slow-Shutter Crowd Drag, Over-Under Split Shot, Documentary Typology Portrait, Low Oblique Aerial, Frame-Within-Frame Observational, Humanist Reportage, High-ISO Night Reportage. Category now 20.
- Distinctions kept on purpose: Humanist Reportage (soft grey, tender) vs Street Photography (hard contrast, decisive moment); Low Oblique Aerial (angled, horizon) vs Drone Aerial (nadir); High-ISO Night Reportage (no flash) vs Paparazzi and the hard-flash light modifier.

- All 20 DNA blocks were the router template ("light transport, optical artifacts, exposure discipline, atmosphere depth, and camera-response behavior").
- **12 names duplicated pack_01 "2. Lighting Techniques" exactly** (Golden Hour, Blue Hour, Rembrandt Lighting, Neon Noir, God Rays, Silhouette, Split Lighting, Butterfly Lighting, Candlelight, Bioluminescence, Strobe Light, Light Painting), so the library showed two presets with the same name and no stated difference — the review's own diagnosis.

- DNA rewritten for all 20 (version 2) as **cinematic, film-set lighting**: large sources through windows, bounce and negative fill with stated contrast ratios, haze, practicals rigged in frame, film grades. Content and location stay constant, per the review validation.
- Renamed to their cinema variant (old names kept as `styleAnchors` search aliases): Magic Hour Backlight, Blue Hour Cinematography, Cinematic Chiaroscuro Key, Neon Practical Noir, Hazed Window Shafts, Cinematic Silhouette Backdrop, Cinematic Split Key, Old Hollywood Beauty Key, Candlelit Low-Light Cinema, Bioluminescent Fantasy Glow, Club Strobe Stutter (several frozen positions, unlike pack_01's single freeze), Motion Light Trails (moving practicals, unlike pack_01's hand-drawn light painting), and Anamorphic Overflare.
- Kept names: Double Exposure (in-camera superimposition, distinct from pack_01's portrait-specific preset), Bokeh (Macro), Day for Night, Prism Effect, Rim Lighting, Underwater Light, Softbox Studio.
- 3 new briefs per preset framed as film stills; no trams, buses or wet streets.

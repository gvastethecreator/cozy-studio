# pack_02 :: 2. TV And Broadcast — audit

Audited 2026-09-25 from the contact sheet (23 primaries + 1 variant) and full manifests. The category already has 23 presets, so none were added.

## Category-wide defects

- All 23 DNA blocks were the "cinematic-media router" template with a shared "broadcast signal texture, package graphics, safe-frame composition …" filler.
- **Six near-duplicate pairs** produced near-identical cards: 017/121 (same sitcom living room), 018/122 (same two anchors), 024/123 (public access), 025/126 (same infomercial host), 026/124 (same sprint finish), 027/125 (same hurricane radar).
- Treatments imposed presenters, tickers and captions, against the review rule "a video treatment must not add a presenter or fabricated ticker".
- Real names and brands in names or fields: "Hype Williams" (SP02-021), "MTV" (SP02-028), the show title "Home Movies" (SP02-115), "BBC Earth-style" (SP02-030). Several cards show generic stock-anchor faces.
- SP02-022 CCTV duplicated the single-camera CCTV profile in pack_01 (SP01-025).

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 23 (version 2). Two kinds are now explicit:
  - treatments keep the prompt's content and never add a presenter, host, audience or readable caption;
  - graphic formats (bulletin crawl, forecast map, radar, replay breakdown, security quad split, signal break) own the layout, and every band, panel and symbol stays textless.
- The six pairs are separated by mechanism, not deleted:
  - 017 is the three-wall set and flat studio wash; 121 is the tape signal (interlace, dot crawl, chroma bleed).
  - 018 is the network studio light and LED wall; 122 is budget chroma-key compositing with halos and mismatched light.
  - 024 is the amateur community studio; 123 is the character-generator bulletin slide.
  - 025 is the split-screen before/after; 126 is the overlit demo with callout shapes.
  - 026 is the live HD telephoto feed; 124 is the analog telestrator replay.
  - 027 is the composited forecast map; 125 is the pure radar sweep.
- SP02-022 is now a CRT quad split of four security feeds, distinct from pack_01's single-camera CCTV.
- Renamed SP02-021 **Fisheye Glam Music Video**, SP02-028 **90s Grunge Music Video**, SP02-115 **Marker-Edge Improvised Sitcom Cartoon**.
- 3 new briefs per preset (69 total) with invented characters, no repeated subject, no real presenters.

# pack_01 :: 1. Portrait And Studio — audit

Audited 2026-09-24 from the contact sheet of current cards (18 primaries + 3 variants) and full manifests.

## Category-wide defects

- Cards reuse the same two stock models: a bearded man in an olive shirt (SP01-001, 005, 009) and a brunette woman (SP01-010, 069, 079, 080, 081). Presets cannot be told apart by subject.
- No reviewed card briefs existed; cards came from generic legacy briefs ("adult violin maker in a plain dark shirt"), which produced stock-photo sameness.
- DNA text was a template ("acts as a transferable photographic style router: begin from …") repeated across presets, with the same filler phrase "studio subject hierarchy, controlled portrait optics, believable retouching, and intentional backdrop behavior" in every `key_features`. Nothing specified the actual lighting ratios, lens, backdrop or pose that make each style distinct.

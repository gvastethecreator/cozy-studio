# pack_04 :: 6. Technical And Reference Sheets — audit

Audited 2026-09-25 from the contact sheet (6 primaries + 6 variants) and full manifests. The category had 6 presets; 14 were added to reach 20.

## Findings

- All 6 DNA blocks were the router template ("acts as a transferable illustration router"). They listed tags ("Measurements, dimension lines, grid sub-base") instead of drawing conventions.
- The review rule (a reference sheet stays a sheet, with no placeholder gibberish presented as valid technical data) was not stated. Several cards break it:
  - SP04-059-01 is a character poster with decorative HUD shapes, not a HUD.
  - SP04-099-01 is a poster, not a wireframe.
  - SP04-086 carries readable callout words.
  - The blueprint cards (057) carry pseudo-dimension text.
- Inherited negatives fought the profiles. The common `UI overlay` negative contradicts both HUD presets. `fantasy proportion` and `stylized` block creature anatomy plates. `human-centric` and `tiny` fight a size chart that needs a small human benchmark.

## Changes

- DNA rewritten for all 6. One shared profile contract: the preset owns its sheet layout, stays a sheet, and shows every callout as a blank tag, arrow or symbol. Dimensions have blank value gaps, charts have unnumbered ticks, and wireframes use grey bars in place of text.
- SP04-059 is a first-person view with a luminous HUD overlaid. SP04-099 is a set of grey-box low-fidelity layout alternatives. `UI overlay` is dropped on both.
- SP04-094 is layered écorché plates (surface, muscle, skeleton) and may show creatures. SP04-100 is a smallest-to-largest silhouette lineup with a human benchmark.
- 14 new profiles:
  - Patent Figure Plate
  - Botanical Dissection Plate
  - Field Guide Species Plate
  - Pose Library Sheet
  - Expression Model Sheet
  - Garment Pattern Layout
  - Wordless Assembly Steps
  - Poché Section Drawing
  - Heraldry Tincture Sheet
  - Celestial Atlas Plate
  - Three-View Recognition Chart
  - Perspective Construction Drawing
  - Motion Cycle Linkage Study
  - Archaeological Find Drawing
- Overlaps checked:
  - Blueprint (SP10-076, SP11-033) is a subject-in-blueprint style; SP04-057 is a multi-view drafting sheet.
  - Exploded View (SP03-064), Toy-Scale Sectional Cutaway (SP07-074), Scientific Botanical (SP04-030) and Character Design (T-Pose) (SP03-049) are rendered images, not these sheet conventions.
  - The Concept Art deliverables do not overlap the new sheets: Character Sheet turnaround, Weapon Design, Prop Design and Silhouette Iteration Sheet.
- 60 briefs with no subject repeated. Every brief ends with a no-readable-text clause, and several explicitly forbid numerals.
